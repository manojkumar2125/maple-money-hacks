Make the waiver work

A $4,000 balance can erase about $203.40 a year on a common mid-tier plan. BMO has the largest straight-cash headline shown at up to $900; Scotiabank’s bundled headline reaches up to $1,000.

5.1% fee-saving equivalent on $4,000

Price the premium perks

A $6,000 balance can waive about $30 monthly, while card rebates and travel features may add value.

About $360/year saved = 6% on $6,000
The golden rule: the bonus is a one-time sugar rush; the fee structure is the daily diet. Choose the fee you can live with first, bonus second.

Read this before switching

A large headline is not the same thing as easy money. Treat every condition as part of the price.

Complete every action.

Payroll deposits, automatically scheduled payments and bill payments may need to continue for months.

Check clawback terms.

CIBC may reclaim its bonus if the qualifying direct deposit is removed within a year.

Confirm new-client eligibility.

Prior chequing-account history at the same bank can make you ineligible.

Price the effort.

Switching banks takes work. Make sure the ongoing account still fits after the bonus is gone.
