01 — What it does

Room tracking, done for you

Most Canadians guess their contribution room — and guessing wrong gets expensive. This tracker subtracts every payday from your Jan 1 room and warns you first.

🏠

FHSA room-runs-out projection

FHSA room is $8,000 a year to a $40,000 lifetime cap. The tracker shows which pay your room runs out, and when to redirect that money to your TFSA instead.

⚠️

OVER ROOM flags

Red ⚠ OVER ROOM warnings appear before you over-contribute to a TFSA, FHSA or RRSP — the mistake CRA penalizes at 1% per month on the excess.

👥

Built for two people

Partners each get their own TFSA, FHSA and RRSP room tracked side by side — because CRA room is per person, never shared. Single? Leave Person 2 blank.

🪣

Cash buckets

Emergency fund, car repairs, family, vacations — separate goals, one savings account. Set a target and a per-pay amount for each, and watch them fill.

✅

Payday check-off

26 payday rows with your planned amounts pre-filled. Tick Done, and cumulative totals and remaining room update themselves.

📈

Progress at a glance

Dashboard progress bars toward your home, emergency and retirement goals, plus RRSP progress toward the $60,000 Home Buyers' Plan target.

02 — How it works

Set up in 10 minutes

Download and open

Open the file in Excel, or upload it to Google Sheets. A Start Here sheet walks you through everything.

Enter your Jan 1 numbers

Your names, starting balances and CRA room for each account — yellow cells only. Find your exact room in CRA My Account.

Add your per-pay amounts

What you contribute to each TFSA, FHSA and RRSP every payday. The tracker projects your full year instantly.

Tick off each payday

That's the whole routine. Room left, totals and goal progress stay current on their own.

Review twice a year

Jan 1 for fresh room, Dec 31 to close the year. Automate the contributions in your bank or broker app and let payday do the work.
