The golden rule: Promos expire in 3–5 months, then rates drop to almost nothing. Set a reminder, move your money, and chase the next promo.

Read this before chasing a rate

A high rate only wins when you qualify, can leave the money where required, and know what happens next.

The promo rate is temporary.

Know what the rate drops to after the offer period.

New-client offers exclude existing customers.

Check the eligibility dates and prior-account rules.

Caps and tiers change your real return.

The eligible-deposit cap is $200K at Simplii and $1M at BMO and CIBC.

Notice accounts trade access for yield.

Withdrawals can be locked for 10–30 days, so keep emergency cash instant-access.

Membership rates have a price.

Neo’s paid tiers cost a monthly fee. Do the math on your balance before upgrading.
