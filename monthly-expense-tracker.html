01 — What it does

Your phone captures. The sheet explains.

Daily expense apps are great at recording and terrible at perspective. This tracker is the perspective layer: one page per year, every category totalled by month, and a review sheet that tells you what to talk about.

📱

Daily capture stays in your app

Keep using any expense-manager app you like for the daily 30-second logging. The sheet never asks you to type individual purchases — only each category's monthly total.

🗓️

One page, twelve months

Income and 11 expense groups, month by month, with a running YEAR column — so a December splurge and a January reset sit side by side and patterns jump out.

🏆

Top 3 categories, automatically

The Month-End Review names your three biggest expense categories every month. No digging, no guessing — the conversation starts at the biggest number.

💬

Built for a money date

Each month ends with two boxes to fill in together: one thing to trim, and one win. Small, specific, and done in 20 minutes — not a line-by-line audit.

🇨🇦

Canadian rows included

RRSP, TFSA, FHSA, CPP, EI, income taxes and an emergency-fund row live right inside the budget, because in Canada saving is a monthly line item, not an afterthought.

💰

Savings count as money out

Money moved to investments leaves your spending account, so the sheet counts it as out — then shows an "of which: savings & investments" line so you can see the good outflows separately.

02 — The ritual

Three steps, once a month

Capture daily (30 seconds)

Track every expense, every day, in any expense-manager app on your phone. Use the same category names as the sheet, so month-end takes minutes instead of detective work.

Total monthly (10 minutes)

At month-end, set the year on the Monthly Budget sheet and drop each category's monthly total into the matching yellow cell. Totals, the YEAR column and what you kept calculate themselves.

Review together (20 minutes)

Open Month-End Review with your partner. Ask three questions: What was our biggest category — and was it worth it? What is ONE thing we'll trim next month? What is ONE win? Write down the trim and the win, then close the sheet until next month.
